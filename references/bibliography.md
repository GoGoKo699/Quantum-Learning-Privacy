# Bibliography

Version-specific theorem locators in [Related work](related-work.md) refer to the versions below. Publisher information and the mathematical version used are stated separately.

## AK2025

**Armando Angrisani and Elham Kashefi.** *Quantum Local Differential Privacy and Quantum Statistical Query Model.* IEEE Transactions on Information Theory **71**, issue 5 (2025). [DOI: 10.1109/TIT.2025.3552671](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2025.3552671). Theorem locators: [arXiv:2203.03591v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03591v2), 21 August 2023.

## NPLB2026

**Seung-Hyun Nam, Hyun-Young Park, Si-Hyeon Lee, and Joonwoo Bae.** *Quantum Advantage in Locally Differentially Private Hypothesis Testing.* Accepted to IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, as recorded by [arXiv:2501.10152v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10152v4), 30 June 2026. Comparison locators use v4.

## NBL2026

**Theshani Nuradha, Sujeet Bhalerao, and Felix Leditzky.** *Privacy-Utility Tradeoffs in Quantum Information Processing.* [arXiv:2602.10510v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10510v1), 11 February 2026. [DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2602.10510](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.10510).

## BNL2026

**Sujeet Bhalerao, Theshani Nuradha, and Felix Leditzky.** *High quantum local differential privacy breaks entanglement.* [arXiv:2609.13418v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13418v1), 11 September 2026. Theorem III.1 gives the universal entanglement-breaking threshold; Theorem V.1 gives the learning simulation.

## HLSW2004

**Patrick Hayden, Debbie Leung, Peter W. Shor, and Andreas Winter.** *Randomizing quantum states: Constructions and applications.* Communications in Mathematical Physics **250**, 371–391 (2004). [DOI: 10.1007/s00220-004-1087-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-004-1087-6). [arXiv:quant-ph/0307104v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0307104v3), 15 June 2004.

## Aubrun2009

**Guillaume Aubrun.** *On almost randomizing channels with a short Kraus decomposition.* Communications in Mathematical Physics **288**, 1103–1116 (2009). [DOI: 10.1007/s00220-008-0695-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-008-0695-y). Theorem locators: [arXiv:0805.2900v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.2900v2), 12 June 2008.

## LGY2026

**Chenjian Li, Ji Guan, and Mingsheng Ying.** *Entanglement-Assisted Quantum Differential Privacy.* [arXiv:2611.25775v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25775v1), 19 November 2026. Definition 3 gives the equal-reference-marginal privacy condition.

## LW2024

**Cécilia Lancien and Andreas Winter.** *Approximating quantum channels by completely positive maps with small Kraus rank.* Quantum **8**, 1320 (2024). [DOI: 10.22331/q-2024-04-30-1320](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2024-04-30-1320).

## Meckes2019

**Elizabeth S. Meckes.** *The Random Matrix Theory of the Classical Compact Groups.* Cambridge University Press (2019). [Author's text](https://case.edu/artsci/math/mwmeckes/elizabeth/Haar_book.pdf), Section 5.4, especially Theorem 5.16 for Haar concentration.

## CCHL2021

**Sitan Chen, Jordan Cotler, Hsin-Yuan Huang, and Jerry Li.** *Exponential separations between learning with and without quantum memory.* FOCS 2021. [arXiv:2111.05881v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05881v2), 18 November 2021. [DOI: 10.1109/FOCS52979.2021.00063](https://doi.org/10.1109/FOCS52979.2021.00063).

## Montanaro2019

**Ashley Montanaro.** *Pretty simple bounds on quantum state discrimination.* [arXiv:1908.08312v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.08312v2), 7 November 2019. Theorem 1 and Lemma 3 give the mixed-state PGM bounds used here; its fidelity convention is root fidelity.

## Cheng2025

**Hao-Chung Cheng, Nilanjana Datta, Nana Liu, Theshani Nuradha, Robert Salzmann, and Mark M. Wilde.** *An invitation to the sample complexity of quantum hypothesis testing.* npj Quantum Information **11**, 94 (2025). [DOI: 10.1038/s41534-025-00980-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-025-00980-8). Comparison locators: [arXiv:2403.17868v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17868v2), 26 April 2024, Theorem 11 and Appendix J.

## KS2014

**Robert Koenig and John A. Smolin.** *How to efficiently select an arbitrary Clifford group element.* Journal of Mathematical Physics **55**, 122202 (2014). [DOI: 10.1063/1.4903507](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4903507). [arXiv:1406.2170v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2170v1), 9 June 2014.

## LabelNoiseSource

**Ruge Lin.** *Label Noise Separates Quantum Sample Advantage from Efficient Learning.* Public scientific account in [Quantum-Learning-Label-Noise](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Learning-Label-Noise/tree/f305698454964e101311b3a337efe49c71808cff), revision `f305698454964e101311b3a337efe49c71808cff`. The model, collective-statistics chapter, individual-measurements chapter, and related-work account supply the source and comparison discussed here.

## Watrous2018

**John Watrous.** *The Theory of Quantum Information.* Cambridge University Press (2018). [Author's book page](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/). Chapters 2–3 give channels, measurements, norms, fidelity and discrimination; Chapter 5 treats entropy and Holevo information; Chapter 7 treats invariant measures and second moments.
